FAQ

Got questions?

We've tried to cover the most common ones. Can't find what you're looking for? Reach out to Olivia or Caroline.

Check-in and check-out
Check-in is at 15:00, check-out at 12:00. Just say your name and Encube at reception — that's all you need.
Breakfast hours
Breakfast is served 08:00–10:00.
What's covered by Encube?
Hotel, flights, travel costs, taxi, breakfast, lunch and dinner are all covered by Encube. With that said, please be mindful about your expenses — share a taxi with others if you're heading into town. There's also a bus into town, and it's walkable too.
How do expenses and reimbursements work?
Submit expenses with valid receipts (including VAT). Mark the expense in Kleer with "offsite". Approved expenses will be reimbursed mid-month / in connection with the regular payroll run.
Common spaces at the hotel
All common areas are available for work — rooftop terrace, pool, lobby and living room. If you have external meetings, take those in your hotel room. And if you're working in shared spaces, be mindful of confidentiality. We have most of the hotel to ourselves, but there are still a few other guests around.
Who should I contact if I have questions?
Before reaching out, check if the answer is already on this page or in our WhatsApp group. If you still can't find it — Olivia or Caroline 💪🏼
